About

Hi, I'm Maquita.

I specialize in creating visually stunning and user-friendly digital and print material for nonprofits, churches, and mission-driven organizations. From concept to implementation, I reflect your unique identity and make it easier for your community to connect with you online.

With experience across platforms including Shopify, Drupal, Wix, Squarespace, WordPress, and Realm, I bring both design sensibility and technical know-how to every project. I don't just build websites — I build relationships, learning your voice, your brand, and your goals so the end result feels authentically yours.

Every organization deserves a digital presence that matches the quality of their work. Whether you're a church looking to modernize, a nonprofit expanding your reach, or a small business ready to launch — I'll meet you where you are and help you get where you're going.

Process

How we work together.

A simple, clear process designed to respect your time and deliver a website you're proud of.

01

Discovery Session

We meet virtually to confirm direction, priorities, and goals for your new website. I review your current platform and its capabilities, then turn what we discuss into actual strategy work so you can see what to expect.

Virtual meeting · Typically 1-2 weeks
02

Develop the Website

I outline the site architecture and get your approval, then develop the content — writing fresh copy, copyediting existing text, and building out each page. Nothing goes live without your sign-off.

Approvals at every stage · Typically 3-4 weeks
03

Launch

The final step — we set up all integrations, run thorough QA testing, and launch your new site. We'll meet virtually to walk through everything and make sure it's exactly what you envisioned.

Virtual meeting · Final testing · Typically 2-3 weeks
04

Ongoing Support

After launch, I'm here for monthly maintenance, content updates, analytics reporting, and social media management. Your website stays as active and current as your organization.

Monthly meetings · Continuous improvement
My Commitment

A partnership, not just a project.

If I decide to step away from monthly obligations, I will work with your team to bring them up to speed on generally updating the site and other digital platforms — and I'll stay for six weeks until you can find a replacement. Your organization will never be left without support.
